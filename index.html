About Our Smart City

Nestled on the sacred banks of the Krishna River and surrounded by the scenic Eastern Ghats, Vijayawada is transitioning into a future-ready, sustainable metropolis blending ancient heritage with cutting-edge technology.

Transforming the Cultural Capital into a High-Tech Smart Metropolis

The Smart Vijayawada Initiative combines sustainable urban planning with cutting-edge civic technologies. From sensor-monitored water networks and smart power grids to intelligent traffic signals and digitized citizen portals, we are creating a cleaner, safer, and highly efficient city for every resident and visitor.

Digital Infrastructure

Gigabit public optical fiber rings, ubiquitous civic Wi-Fi, and AI-enabled urban operations.

Sustainable Development

Solar-powered municipal facilities, automated rainwater harvesting, and waste-to-energy power conversion.

Modern Technology & Safety

Facial recognition safety hubs, adaptive street illumination, and rapid emergency dispatch systems.

Our Vision for Tomorrow

"To establish Vijayawada as the benchmark of inclusive, livable, climate-resilient, and technologically advanced smart cities in South India by 2030."

Iconic Landmarks

Important City Locations

Explore the historical monuments, sacred shrines, scenic river wonders, and bustling hubs that define the soul and vibrancy of Vijayawada.

Engineering Marvel

Prakasam Barrage

An iconic 1,223-meter-long road bridge and barrage spanning the sacred Krishna River. It creates a magnificent lake backdrop and illuminates with smart LED architectural lights every evening.

Krishna River, Vijayawada 4.8
Sacred Heritage

Sri Kanaka Durga Temple

Revered ancient hill temple atop Indrakeeladri Hill dedicated to Goddess Kanaka Durga. Features panoramic views of the entire city, smart queue tokens, and modern ghat road accessibility.

Indrakeeladri Hill 4.9
Eco Tourism

Bhavani Island

One of India’s largest river islands, spanning 133 acres in the middle of Krishna River. A vibrant hub for water sports, mangrove nature walks, luxury river camping, and family entertainment.

Krishna River Midstream 4.7
National Transit Hub

Vijayawada Railway Station (BZA)

A bustling transit nerve center connecting Northern and Southern India. Modernized with smart escalators, automated luggage sanitizers, solar roofs, and air-conditioned executive lounges.

Station Road, Central 4.6
Historic Memorial

Gandhi Hill

The first Gandhi memorial in India built upon a 500-foot hill, featuring a 52-foot stupa, a modern planetarium, sound-and-light history shows, and scenic views overlooking the railway yard.

Tarapet, Old Town 4.5
7th Century Heritage

Undavalli Caves

Exquisite 7th-century monolithic rock-cut cave monuments carved out of solid sandstone hills, showcasing an iconic colossal reclining statue of Lord Vishnu and fine architectural relics.

Undavalli, Krishna Bank 4.8
Sustainable Progress

Smart City Initiatives

Pioneering ecological and tech-driven interventions to make Vijayawada climate-resilient, energy-efficient, and digitally inclusive for generations ahead.

Green Energy & Solar Transition

Equipping all municipal buildings, government schools, and reservoirs with rooftop solar panels targeting 35MW clean electricity generation by 2028.

Zero Emission Target

Citywide Gigabit Fiber Grid

High-bandwidth optical fiber backbone connecting administrative offices, civic health clinics, and security sensors with 99.9% uptime and low latency.

Gigabit Backbone

Swachh Krishna & Air Quality Mission

Continuous ambient air quality monitoring systems (CAAQMS) deployed across high-traffic zones paired with comprehensive mechanized road sweepers and bio-remediation.

Pure Air Index

Smart Traffic Optimization

Dynamic variable message signs (VMS) alerting drivers about lane closures, congestion hotspots, and alternative bypass routes across Kanaka Durga flyover.

25% Congestion Cut

Resilient & Sustainable Infrastructure

Stormwater drainage retrofits, underground utility ducting, and flood-resilient embankments along Budameru and Krishna canals to mitigate extreme weather hazards.

Flood-Proofing

Water Conservation & Recycling

100% wastewater treatment through advanced sewage treatment plants (STPs), with recycled greywater channeled for urban gardening, median greenery, and industrial cooling.
